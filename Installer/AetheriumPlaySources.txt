AETHERIUM PLAY DOWNLOAD SOURCES AND VERIFICATION

This setup is intentionally small. It does not embed the historical Dark
Majesty game installer or client.exe. Those files come from the disclosed
ACCPP community-hosted MEGA collection and are accepted only after
exact size and SHA-256 verification.

The historical installer and client.exe are hosted by the ACCPP community.

5. DGVOODOO 2 (GRAPHICS WRAPPER)

Legacy Dark Majesty needs a DirectDraw wrapper on modern Windows. Aetherium
Play includes individual dgVoodoo 2 binary components by Dege (not Aetherium
source code):

  DDraw.dll
  D3DImm.dll
  dgVoodoo.conf  (dgVoodoo 2.87.x)

Upstream: https://dege.freeweb.hu/dgVoodoo2/

dgVoodoo remains the property of its author. Aetherium ships only the files
needed for this game install. Full attribution and redistribution notes ship
with the product documentation (dgVoodoo-NOTICE.md).
